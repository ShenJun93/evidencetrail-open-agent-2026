04 / Working product, not independently validated operational advice

Claim Ledger & Why?

Deterministic, source-pinned historical replay

The source-bound Claim Ledger checks source IDs, publisher, official URL, excerpt digest, typed value and issuer/date policy before choosing admissible claims. Florida date-only references are held until a curator-defined end-of-local-day boundary, rather than inventing a publication hour.

The separate Why? counterfactual screen uses the same curated seven-record sample to simulate deleting one source. At 11:00 UTC, deleting Florida EO #215 changes the selected order from 51 to 35 counties; removing NOAA advisory #8 makes its wind claim abstain. At 15:00 UTC, removing NOAA #10 changes 155 to 125 mph.

Simulated deletion is not a real publisher retraction. Strict original-publication policy correctly abstains without independent snapshot evidence. Neither this view nor its what-if operations call NVIDIA or Zetaris.
