## Short description (ready to adapt)
EvidenceTrail is a research-oriented evidence-audit agent that explains not only why it reached an answer, but what evidence could have been considered at a specific historical time. Its Collector applies an as-of source filter, a constrained Decision Agent proposes a result, and an independent Auditor verifies source identities and citations. Counterfactual Lab removes individual inputs, recomputes the decision, and identifies necessary supporting evidence. When the publication history of a source is uncertain, strict mode abstains instead of retrospectively claiming it was known. The historical Typhoon Yagi case is explicitly a research replay, **not** an operational warning system; one internal-capacity scenario is FICTIONAL.

## Problem / target users / impact (Impact 30)
Evidence analysts, safety researchers and compliance investigators can be misled by AI-generated explanations that cite documents that were available only later. They need inspectable evidence timelines, explicit missing facts and a dependable refusal to overstate historical knowledge. EvidenceTrail's output makes those failure modes visible and testable. **Impact is hypothesized, not measured by a field study.** Future use: insurance investigation, audit replay, scientific literature review and incident postmortems.
